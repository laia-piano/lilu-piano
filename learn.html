Before you purchase

We strongly recommend that every customer carefully watch our introduction video in full. Commercial fraud is a serious problem in the piano market in Singapore, and we will help you avoid overspending on low-quality pianos.

U1 or U3？It's a trap!

If you are still obsessing over the “difference” between the U1 and U3, you are already being pulled into a classic sales trap. This is the most common distraction used to mislead buyers of Yamaha pianos, and in markets like Singapore, many piano dealers deliberately steer customers in this direction while avoiding the real, critical factors.

If you genuinely want to avoid wasting money, you must take the time to watch our educational video in full. This is the only way to protect yourself from overpaying and making the wrong decision.
